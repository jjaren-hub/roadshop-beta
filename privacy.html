Operator: [legal entity, postal address, contact email to be confirmed]. Effective date: [launch date]. This draft requires legal and owner review.

Data collected

Account identity and email (including Google sign-in), business profiles, user-entered bookkeeping records, customer/vehicle data, receipts, documents, support requests and optional screenshots, subscription status and provider identifiers, limited feature activity and error counts. Stripe will collect payment details on its own hosted pages; EarnKeep does not store full card numbers. Do not include credentials or unnecessary personal information in support messages.

Purpose and sharing

We use data to deliver bookkeeping, reports, synchronization, security, support and billing. Hosting/authentication use Supabase; static hosting uses GitHub Pages. When separately enabled and expressly requested, selected documents may be sent to a configured OCR provider; cloud OCR is currently disabled. We do not send the complete ledger to an AI model for deterministic calculations. Provider retention and international transfer terms must be confirmed before launch. Authorized administrators see operational aggregates and voluntarily submitted support content, not unrestricted financial workspaces.

Retention, export and deletion

Users can export their cloud workspace data from Billing. Cancellation does not automatically erase records. Account deletion requests can be submitted in Support; identity verification, billing cancellation, required legal retention, backup expiration and processor records may affect the process. Actual retention periods and backup deletion schedules must be finalized before launch. Encrypted offline drafts are stored in the browser; the passphrase is not recoverable. Clearing browser storage can erase unsynced drafts.

Security and rights

Workspace access is owner-scoped, with server-controlled billing and administrator roles. No security measure guarantees absolute protection. Depending on location, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive copies of personal information. Contact support for requests. Business users are responsible for lawful handling of their customers’ data. The operator must review applicable privacy laws, subprocessors, notice and consent requirements, and breach response before publication.
