This Privacy Policy explains how EarnKeep collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use our web application. EarnKeep is built so that your financial records stay on your own device. This Policy explains exactly what reaches our servers, and when.

1. INTRODUCTION AND SCOPE

1.1 What this Policy covers. This Policy applies to personal information processed through the EarnKeep web application at https://www.earnkeep.app and related communications (the "Service"). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in our Terms of Service.

1.2 What it does not cover. This Policy does not cover third-party websites or services that we do not control, including Google, Stripe and the email services you use, which have their own privacy policies.

1.3 Business users. The Service is designed for business owners and independent contractors managing their own business finances. This Policy applies to you as an individual user and to any individual whose information you enter into the Service, such as your Customers.

2. WHO WE ARE AND HOW TO CONTACT US

2.1 Controller. The Service is operated by Jaren Jorgensen, a sole proprietor based in Utah, doing business as EarnKeep ("EarnKeep", "we", "us" or "our"). We are responsible for personal information we process for our own purposes as described in this Policy.

2.2 Contact details. You can contact us about privacy at:

3. OUR LOCAL-FIRST DESIGN

3.1 Records stay on your device. The income, expense, bill, savings, forecast, Quote, trucking and mechanic records you create are stored in the browser or on the device you use to access the Service ("Local Data"). We do not receive these records in normal operation. If you contact us and choose to share them, we receive only what you provide.

3.2 Backups. When you export a .json backup file or a PDF or CSV report, the file is saved where you choose. We do not receive a copy unless you choose to send it to us, for example for support.

3.3 No cloud synchronization. The Service does not offer cloud synchronization of financial or business records. These records are not stored in our cloud database and are not automatically available on other devices. Supabase is used for authentication and account-related information, not financial record storage.

3.4 What we see. In normal operation, account and billing information we may access includes your name, email address, Account status and the subscription and billing details described in Section 8.2. Technical information is described in Section 9. We may also receive information you voluntarily share with us for support. We do not have routine access to Local Data.

4. INFORMATION YOU PROVIDE TO US

4.1 Account information. When you create an Account we collect your name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider, Supabase, and are stored in hashed form. We do not see your password.

4.2 Google sign-in. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier with us. We request only basic profile access. We do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or other Google account data.

4.3 Communications. If you contact us, we collect the information you choose to provide, such as your message, any screenshots or files you attach and your contact details.

4.4 Preferences. We collect account and subscription preferences as needed to provide the Service. Financial settings and record-related notification preferences are stored locally.

5. FINANCIAL RECORDS AND BUSINESS CONTENT

5.1 Types of records. Your records may include income and expense entries, amounts, dates, vendors, categories, recurring bills and due dates, savings envelopes, forecasts, estimated tax inputs, mileage and load information, settlement figures, Quotes and invoices, and notes.

5.2 Where they are processed. These records are processed and stored locally on your device. We do not transmit them to our servers or AI service providers as part of normal operation. If you choose to send records to us for support, we handle that information as described in Sections 4.3 and 16.

5.3 Do not enter unnecessary sensitive information. The Service does not need Social Security numbers, full bank or card account numbers, tax identification numbers, health information or login credentials for other services. Please do not enter them in notes, Quotes, attachments.

6. INFORMATION ABOUT YOUR CUSTOMERS AND OTHER THIRD PARTIES

6.1 Customer information. If you use mechanic tools or Quotes and invoices, you may enter information about your Customers, such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, addresses, vehicle details and job descriptions.

6.2 Your role. You decide what Customer information to enter and how to use it. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to collect and share it and for giving your Customers any notice the law requires. Customer records remain Local Data unless you choose to share them, for example when you export a Quote and send it to your Customer or contact us for support.

6.3 Customer requests. If a Customer contacts us about their information, we will refer the request to you where we can identify you, and will assist you as reasonably required.

7. RECEIPTS AND DOCUMENTS

7.1 Manual attachments. You may attach receipt images or documents to your records where that feature is available. Attachments are stored as Local Data and are not uploaded to our servers in normal operation.

8. PAYMENT INFORMATION

8.1 Stripe processes payments. Subscription payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details are entered into and stored by Stripe. We do not receive or store your full card number or security code.

8.2 What we receive. Stripe provides us with your billing name, email address, the type and last four digits of your card, its expiry date, billing country or postal code, subscription status, and payment history, so we can manage your Subscription.

8.3 Stripe's role. Stripe processes payment information under its own privacy policy, including for fraud prevention and to meet its legal obligations.

9. INFORMATION COLLECTED AUTOMATICALLY

9.1 Technical data. When you use the Service, our hosting, security and database providers automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, pages and features requested, date and time of access, and error and security logs.

9.2 Purpose. We use this information to deliver the Service, keep it secure, prevent abuse and fraud, diagnose faults and understand overall performance.

9.3 No advertising tracking. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or advertising tracking technologies in the Service.

10. COOKIES, LOCAL STORAGE AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

10.1 Browser storage. The Service uses your browser's local storage and similar technologies to store Local Data on your device and to keep you signed in. This storage is essential to how the Service works. If you clear it, your Local Data and sign-in state will be deleted from that browser.

10.2 Essential cookies. We and our providers use cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary to authenticate you, keep your session secure, remember your preferences, process payments and protect the Service against bots and attacks. Cloudflare and Stripe may set security and fraud-prevention cookies for these purposes.

10.3 Your controls. You can block or delete cookies and browser storage through your browser settings. If you do, parts of the Service will not work and Local Data stored in that browser may be lost.

10.4 Changes. If we introduce non-essential cookies, such as analytics cookies, we will update this Policy and provide any choices required by law before using them.

15. DATA SECURITY

15.1 Our measures. We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encryption of data in transit using HTTPS, access controls that limit who can access production systems, and reliance on providers that maintain their own security programs.

15.2 Your part. Local Data is only as secure as your device and browser. Use a strong password, lock your device, keep your browser up to date, and avoid using the Service on shared or public computers. Store .json backup files securely, because they contain your records in readable form.

15.3 No guarantee. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If we become aware of a security incident affecting your personal information, we will notify you and any authorities as required by applicable law.

16. DATA RETENTION

16.1 General rule. We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes in this Policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

16.2 Specific periods. Our retention approach is as follows:

(a) Account information: while your Account is active and for up to 90 days after deletion, then deleted, except as stated below.

(b) Billing records: kept for as long as required for tax, accounting and legal purposes.

(c) Security and technical logs: kept for a limited period consistent with our providers' log retention settings and security needs.

(d) Support communications: up to 2 years after the matter is closed.

16.3 Local Data. Local Data stays on your device until you delete it, clear your browser storage or it is otherwise lost. We cannot delete Local Data for you.
