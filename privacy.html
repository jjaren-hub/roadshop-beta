Technical draft for local-only beta; not the final legal policy. The production site may still use the previous cloud workspace architecture until the verified release is promoted.

Data collected

Account identity and email (including Google sign-in), authentication and security metadata, subscription status and provider identifiers when enabled, and support messages you voluntarily submit. Business profiles, bookkeeping records, customer and vehicle data, receipts and documents entered in this beta stay in browser storage; they are not automatically synchronized to EarnKeep servers. Do not include financial records or customer data in support messages. Stripe will collect payment details on its own hosted pages; EarnKeep does not store full card numbers. Do not include credentials or unnecessary personal information in support messages.

Purpose and sharing

We use account data for authentication, security, support and billing. Supabase provides account authentication; GitHub Pages hosts beta and Cloudflare Pages is intended for production. Financial calculations, reports and document storage occur on your device. Cloud OCR and automatic financial-screen activity reporting are disabled in this beta. Provider retention and international transfer terms must be confirmed before launch. Support administrators may see voluntarily submitted support content and permitted account/billing aggregates. Legacy pre-launch test data and backend permissions require review before the production switch.

Retention, export and deletion

Users can download and verify a complete local backup in Backup & Recovery, optionally encrypt it with a password, and restore it on another browser or device. Optional folder backups run only on supported browsers with permission while the app is open. Backup files are saved to a location selected by the user, not EarnKeep servers. Browser data can be erased or evicted, and EarnKeep cannot recover local records without a backup. Cancellation or account deletion does not automatically clear local financial records. Local records can be deleted separately in Backup & Recovery after a verified recovery backup and explicit confirmation. Account deletion requests can be submitted in Support; identity verification, billing cancellation, required legal retention, backup expiration and processor records may affect the process. Actual retention periods and backup deletion schedules must be finalized before launch. Encrypted offline drafts are stored in the browser; the passphrase is not recoverable. Clearing browser storage can erase unsynced drafts.

Security and rights

Local workspaces are separated by signed-in account in browser storage. The main device database is not independently encrypted by EarnKeep; protect the device and browser profile. Billing and administrator access remain server-controlled. No security measure guarantees absolute protection. Depending on location, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive copies of personal information. Contact support for requests. Business users are responsible for lawful handling of their customers’ data. The operator must review applicable privacy laws, subprocessors, notice and consent requirements, and breach response before publication.
