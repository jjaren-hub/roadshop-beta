Privacy by design

Your business is your business.

EarnKeep stores your business and financial records in your browser on your device, rather than uploading your books to an EarnKeep financial database.

Online services handle sign-in, account management and subscription authorization. Financial backups are saved to a location you choose.

Know how your data is stored

On your device

Transactions, receipts, quotes, invoices and business records.

Online account services

Sign-in, account and subscription information.

Keep an external backup.

Clearing browser data or losing a device can erase local records. Sign-in alone cannot recover them. The main local database is not independently encrypted by EarnKeep.

A few things to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything?

EarnKeep works in supported browsers on mobile and desktop. You can also install it as a web app where supported.

Can I move my records to a new device?

Yes. Download and verify a complete backup, sign in to the same account on the other device, and restore the file. Your books do not automatically sync across devices.

Can EarnKeep file my taxes?

No. EarnKeep helps organize records and estimate taxes. Verify your information and consult a qualified tax professional before filing.

Can I work without internet?

Previously verified accounts may access cached records offline for a limited period. Sign-in, subscription services and some browser features need a connection. The app also supports encrypted pending offline entries.
